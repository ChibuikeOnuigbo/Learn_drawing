# Skill-test examples

These are authored, reproducible tests of the rebuilt MCP surface. They are original exercises informed by the supplied reference study, not copies of the reference films. Technical QA and artistic review are reported separately; see `reports/REBUILD_VALIDATION.md` for the measured summary.

## 01 — 2D moonlight (`01-2d-moonlight/`)

**Skill:** asset-based 2D character animation, layered parallax, facial expression, focal FX.

- Painted observatory background (`observatory.jpg`, original AI-generated plate).
- Nested SVG cut-out rig (`character.svg`): body/head pivots, connected arms/legs, eye/blink layers, expression states.
- Independent orb halo/core, 26 particle sparks, foreground parallax silhouette, original synthesized music.
- `scene.json` is the editable timeline; `rig-frames/` is a regenerable raster intermediate (gitignored). Run `scripts/build_showcase.py --stage author` to rebuild it.
- Render: `render/video.mp4`, 1280×720, 24 fps, 240 frames / 10.0 s. `qa.json` reports technical pass.

**Artistic note:** substantially more constructed than a flat image translation, but still a stylized vector cartoon, not reference-film anime parity.

## 02 — Semantic reaction timing (`02-semantic-reactions/`)

**Skill:** generated test speech → local Whisper word times → intent classification → correct meme at the correct second.

| Clip | Trigger word | ASR word time | Cue frame @30fps | Asset |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `brain/` | “all.” | 1.20 s | 36 | Squidward brain-erase GIF (actual eraser gag) |
| `disaster/` | “messed” | 4.84 s | 145 | original anime disaster illustration |
| `victory/` | “worked.” | 2.54 s | 76 | original anime victory illustration |
| `victory/` | “comeback” | 4.92 s | 148 | original anime victory illustration |

Each folder contains `voice.wav`, `narration.json`, `cue-plan.json`, `video.mp4`, and `inspection/qa.json`. The brain cue is placed after the short verbal setup so the punchline lands. GIF frame durations are respected; the bundled copy is cropped only to remove blank source canvas.

**Measured:** cue rounding error ≤ half a frame; audio present; H.264/AAC export. **Review required:** ASR word boundaries are estimates; humor/taste is not scored by the classifier. The Squidward GIF is third-party; replace with licensed/original art before public publication.

## 03 — 3D exercises (`03-3d-easy|simple|medium|hard/`)

**Skill:** editable Blender projects, weight/spacing, humanoid IK, staged cinematography.

| Level | Exercise | Frames | Editable source | QA focus |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| easy | squash/stretch bounce on inlaid dais | 96 | `scene.blend`, `scene3d.json` | impact spacing, export |
| simple | rigged wave on stage | 96 | `scene.blend`, `scene3d.json` | armature, IK reach, foot contact |
| medium | walk through light portals | 120 | `scene.blend`, `scene3d.json` | stance/swing feet, camera track |
| hard | reach/grasp in corridor with iris door + drone | 120 | `scene.blend`, `scene3d.json` | payload attachment, IK error, staged camera |

Renders are 800×450, 24 fps, Cycles CPU with denoising. `rig-qa.json` records evaluated deformed poses, minimum foot vertex height, max IK target error and payload hold error. Difficulty labels describe these exercises, **not** equivalence to the user's advanced reference films.

## 04 — Asset-based short (`04-asset-based-short/`)

**Skill:** multi-shot local assembly, captions, music, cross-engine asset reuse.

Assembles the 2D moonlight render and hard/simple 3D renders into a 9-second 1280×720 edit with timed captions and an original synthesized music bed. `edit.json` is the editable decision list; `qa.json` reports export checks. Source clip audio is intentionally dropped and replaced by the music track.

## 05 — Kinetic typography (`05-kinetic-typography/`)

**Skill:** motion graphics, color roles, readable holds.

Three type cues (`PLAN`, `BUILD`, `REFINE`) enter with opacity/position/scale, hold, then fade. A role palette generated by `color_palette` is stored in `palette.json`; `shot-plan.json` records the exact cue intervals. Render: `render/video.mp4`, 960×540, 24 fps, 96 frames / 4.0 s.

**Review focus:** reading order, hold duration, contrast and clean fade boundaries. This is a typography starter, not a full title-sequence package.

## 06 — 2D walk cycle (`06-2d-walk-cycle/`)

**Skill:** physics/secondary motion, exposure planning, geometric contact checks.

`walk.svg` and `scripts/build_skill_examples.py` generate a 24-frame side-view cycle with two-bone legs, counter-swinging arms, body bob and a treadmill ground. `walk-qa.json` measures stance-foot height error against the ground line; `exposure-sheet.json` labels contact/down/passing/up on twos. Render: `render/video.mp4`, 640×360, 24 fps, 48 frames / 2.0 s. The `frames/` raster sequence is regenerable and gitignored.

**Measured:** max stance-foot y error 0.0 px in the authored cycle. **Review required:** geometric contact is not artistic gait approval; inspect spacing, overlap and silhouette in motion.

## 07 — Particle burst (`07-particle-burst/`)

**Skill:** anticipation, impact, follow-through, authored particle-like motion.

A core squashes before impact, a shockwave expands/fades, and 36 deterministic sparks use sampled drag/gravity-like keys with staggered birth/death. `palette.json` records role colors. Render: `render/video.mp4`, 960×540, 24 fps, 72 frames / 3.0 s.

**Scope:** keyframed geometry, not a particle simulator, fire/fluid solve or learned VFX model. Review trail continuity, fade endpoints and focal hierarchy.

## 08 — Vertical Short (`08-vertical-short/`)

**Skill:** platform delivery, portrait-safe staging.

Portrait-first 540×960 composition with headline, subtitle, bouncing hero, shadow, lower caption and CTA. Essential text is staged away from edges; `delivery-notes.md` records the intent. Render: `render/video.mp4`, 540×960, 24 fps, 120 frames / 5.0 s.

**Review focus:** legibility at phone size and platform overlays. This is a staging proof, not a guarantee of any platform’s current policy.

## 09 — Narrated explainer (`09-narrated-explainer/`)

**Skill:** storyboarding, motion graphics, sound sync, multi-shot editing.

Three clean visual shots are assembled with captions and offline synthetic English narration. `narration.json` contains eSpeak engine word events; `shot-plan.json` records sentence-derived cuts; `edit.json` is the editable decision list. Render: `video.mp4`, 1280×720, 24 fps, 219 frames / 9.125 s, with AAC audio.

**Measured:** audio stream present; caption cues do not overlap; export frame count/dimensions pass. **Review required:** the voice is a synthetic demo, not an expressive human narrator; engine word events are not forced alignment. Replace with supplied final voiceover for production.

## Regeneration

```bash
PATH="/path/to/ffmpeg/bin:$PATH" python scripts/build_showcase.py --stage all
PATH="/path/to/ffmpeg/bin:$PATH" python scripts/build_skill_examples.py
# Select one new example:
python scripts/build_skill_examples.py --only walk
```

Showcase stages: `author`, `2d`, `reactions`, `3d`, `edit`, `all`. Blender is required for 3D. The reaction stage re-renders from the stored `voice.wav` and `narration.json`; re-transcribing requires the pinned Whisper model. `build_skill_examples.py` requires FFmpeg/ffprobe and uses bundled offline eSpeak NG for the explainer. Generated frame sequences and intermediate encode files are gitignored; source projects, cue plans, QA and final videos are retained where size permits.
